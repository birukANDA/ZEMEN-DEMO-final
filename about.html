Who We Are

Your Trusted Steel Partner

Established in 2016, Zemen Trading PLC is a locally owned industrial supply company based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, serving the construction industry with high-quality and reliable materials. We supply premium-grade 75 reinforcement bars, professional-grade wire products, National Cement, staffa rods, plywood, and other essential construction materials.

We serve contractors, building owners, private and public enterprises, developers, and residential projects by providing dependable products and responsive customer service. Our experienced team works closely with customers to understand their specific requirements and provide practical, cost-effective solutions.

Our commitment is built on quality, integrity, professionalism, accountability, and reliable service. Through trusted sourcing, competitive pricing, and dependable delivery, we aim to keep every project supplied and moving forward.

At Zemen Trading PLC, we focus on building lasting customer relationships by delivering quality materials and service you can depend on.
